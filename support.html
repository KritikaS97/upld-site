Support

Email realitycrew.ofc@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can. It helps to say which iPhone you have and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Signing in

UPLD signs you in with your email address and a six-digit code. There is no password. If the code does not arrive within a minute, check your spam folder, then tap Resend. A code works once and expires after ten minutes.

UPLD Pro and billing

Subscriptions are bought through, and billed by, Apple.

  • Cancel or change plan: on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then UPLD.
  • Restore a purchase: in UPLD open Settings and tap Restore purchases.
  • Refunds: Apple handles these at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Exporting

Exported slides are saved to your photo library, numbered in order. If nothing appears, open iPhone Settings, find UPLD, and allow access to Photos.

Your carousels

Carousels are made and stored on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us, so deleting the app deletes them.

Deleting your account

In UPLD open Settings and tap Delete account. This removes your email address and subscription record from our servers. If you have a subscription, cancel it with Apple separately. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.
