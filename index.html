UPLD

Turn your camera roll into a carousel worth swiping.

UPLD is an iPhone app for making photo and video carousels for Instagram. Pick a carousel that is already designed, tap each frame to add your own photos or videos, change the words, and export. Every slide comes out at the right size, in order, ready to post.

Start from a finished design

Monthly dumps, travel recaps, food diaries, celebrations and colour palettes, as carousels for posts, sets for stories and single-slide designs.

Make it yours

Fill any frame with a photo, a video or a colour. Edit every line of text. Add stickers, shapes, frames and drawing. Reorder slides by dragging them.

See it before you post

Slides sit side by side, the way they will on Instagram, with a full-screen preview you can zoom.

UPLD is free to use. UPLD Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks every template and theme, more slides per carousel, longer video clips and high-resolution export.
