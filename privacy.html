Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · Version 1.3

UPLD builds photo and video carousels on your phone. It is made and operated by Kritika Sharma, Bengaluru, India ("UPLD", "we"). This policy explains what the app touches, what it keeps, and what it sends anywhere else.

The short version

UPLD is built to work without collecting your content. Your photos and videos are read on your device, laid out on your device, and exported on your device. They are not uploaded, not analysed on a server, and not used to train anything.

The one thing UPLD holds about you is the email address you sign in with. That is described in full below.

What UPLD accesses

Your photo library. UPLD asks for photo access the first time you open the picker, never at launch. It reads the photos and videos you select in order to lay them out. It also derives, on your device: image dimensions, capture date, where the subject and any faces sit in the frame, and the three most common colours. That information stays on your device with the project and is used only to choose a layout.

Location attached to your photos. If a template uses a {location} label, UPLD asks Apple to convert the coordinates already stored in your photo into a place name. That request goes to Apple, not to UPLD, is cached with the project, and is skipped when you are offline. UPLD never receives your coordinates.

Nothing else. UPLD does not ask for contacts, calendar, microphone, Bluetooth, or precise location. There is no advertising SDK and no tracking SDK, so the app does not ask for tracking permission.

What UPLD collects

Your email address, for your account. UPLD signs you in with an email address and a six-digit code, with no password. To do that we store:

  • your email address and the date the account was created;
  • whether the account has an active UPLD Pro subscription;
  • a sign-in code while it is valid — stored only as a one-way hash, deleted when used and after ten minutes otherwise;
  • session tokens that keep you signed in — stored only as one-way hashes on our side, and in the Keychain on your device;
  • the date your free trial began — stored against a one-way hash of your email address, not the address itself, and on your device in the Keychain.

The email address is used to sign you in, to send you the code, and to carry your subscription to another device. It is not used for marketing and is not shared or sold.

Account data is held on servers operated for us by Fly.io and Supabase, in Singapore. Sign-in emails are sent through Google (Gmail). Those companies process the data on our behalf and only to provide that service.

Purchases. Subscriptions are bought through Apple. UPLD never sees your card or payment details; Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active.

Analytics and crash reports. UPLD does not currently send usage analytics or crash reports to us or to anyone else, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK. The app asks for your choice on first launch; if analytics are ever switched on, they will only run with that consent, they will never include your photos, text, location or email address, and this policy will be updated first.

Instagram

UPLD does not post to Instagram for you and does not connect to your Instagram account. It saves finished slides to your photo library; you post them from Instagram yourself. If a publishing feature is added later, this policy will be updated before it is switched on.

What UPLD never does

  • Sell your data.
  • Share your photos or projects with anyone.
  • Use your content to train machine-learning models.
  • Show you advertising.
  • Track you across other apps or websites.

Keeping and deleting your data

Projects live on your device. Deleting a project deletes it. Deleting the app deletes all of them, along with every cached preview.

Your account. In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. That removes your email address, your subscription record and your session tokens from our servers straight away. Signing out removes the session from the device and our servers but keeps the account. Account data is otherwise kept for as long as the account exists.

One thing is kept after an account is deleted: the date the free trial began, against a one-way hash of the email address. It exists so that the free trial is given once per person, not once per sign-up. The hash cannot be turned back into an email address and is not used for anything else. The same date stays in the Keychain on your device if you delete the app. You can ask for the server record to be removed by emailing us from that address.

To ask what is held, or to have an account deleted when you cannot open the app, email realitycrew.ofc@gmail.com from the address on the account. Requests are answered within 30 days. You can ask for a copy of your data, correction of it, or its deletion.

Security, and what UPLD is not responsible for

UPLD takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the limited data it holds: traffic to the account service runs over HTTPS, sign-in tokens are kept in the device Keychain, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDK is embedded.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and UPLD cannot guarantee absolute security. To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither UPLD nor its proprietor is responsible or liable for loss, corruption or disclosure of data arising from: a breach of a third-party service, including Apple, Instagram and Meta, or any hosting or network provider; unauthorised access to your device, Apple ID or Instagram account not caused by UPLD; your own sharing or publishing of content; or any act of a third party. The limitation of liability in the Terms of Use applies to this policy in full.

Keeping your own backups of photos, videos and exports is your responsibility.

Your rights

You can ask what personal data UPLD holds about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, withdraw consent for photo-library or notification access in iOS Settings at any time, and delete your account from within the app. Deleting the app removes the projects stored on the device. Write to the address below to make a request; UPLD responds within 30 days.

Children

UPLD is not directed at children under 13, and UPLD does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes
